# 09 — Demo Video Script (3:00 max, beats and lines)

## Minute 1 — The Problem
- **(0:00–0:20)** Open on the stat: "Millions of Nigerian children leave primary
  school unable to read fluently. But *why* a child struggles matters more than
  *that* they struggle."
- **(0:20–0:45)** One diagram: silent reading vs. listening. "A child who
  understands when it's read aloud but not when reading alone has a **decoding
  gap** — a completely different problem from a comprehension gap, needing a
  different intervention."
- **(0:45–1:00)** "Pagezy separates the two, in the child's own language, powered
  by Nigeria's own LLM."

## Minute 2 — The Demo
- **(1:00–1:20)** Tab 1: select passage (show a Yoruba one), press Read Aloud.
- **(1:20–1:45)** Answer 3 questions, one deliberately wrong → watch the LLM hint
  appear: "Remember what Adaeze did at the market *before* the rain started…"
- **(1:45–2:00)** Diagnostic card: "Pattern consistent with a decoding gap —
  recommend phonics-focused practice." (Screening language, not diagnosis.)

## Minute 3 — The Engineering Rigor
- **(2:00–2:20)** Switch to Tab 2 leaderboard: "We benchmarked N-ATLAS against
  Qwen2.5 and Llama-3.2 on 1–5 rubric — relevance, grade-fit, cultural fit, and
  safety with auto-fail. Every number is reproducible from the repo."
- **(2:20–2:40)** Compliance verbatim: "The prototype complies with the N-ATLAS
  Research License — capped users, non-commercial, Powered by Awarri — and we
  defer child voice-input to Phase 2."
- **(2:40–3:00)** Close: "In Phase 2, this competition's reach lets us crowdsource
  Nigerian children's speech to fine-tune N-ATLAS ASR — closing a documented gap
  in the national AI stack. Pagezy: built on Nigeria's stack, giving back to it."
